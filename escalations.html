Escalation paths

Who owns the next hour. Complex and VIP issues do not wait in the general queue.

Default path

Customer (APAC / EMEA / AMER)
   │  route to regional on-call first
   ▼
L1 Support Specialist     macros, KB, resets, status, docs
   │  cannot resolve, or SLA at 80%
   ▼
L2 Domain SME             lending · SBA · banking operations
   │  defect, data, or policy gap
   ▼
L3 Engineering / Product  bug, incident, roadmap
   │  revenue, reputation, or examiner risk
   ▼
Executive                 Head of Support + CS + Credit

Special paths

VIPP1 / exec-originated
Skip L1. Assign named support owner and CSM.
RegionAPAC / EMEA / AMER
Route first to the on-call in the customer contact region. Do not park an APAC P2 on the ET queue overnight.
Compliancecomplaint / fair lending
Open a parallel Compliance ticket. Do not wait on L3.

Required fields

  • From level → to level
  • Customer impact in one sentence
  • Customer contact region (APAC / EMEA / AMER)
  • Named owner
  • Next update time in the bank’s local hours
