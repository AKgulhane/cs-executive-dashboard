Escalation paths

Who owns the next hour. Complex and VIP issues do not wait in the general queue.

Default path

Customer
   │
   ▼
L1 Support Specialist     macros, KB, resets, status, docs
   │  cannot resolve, or SLA at 80%
   ▼
L2 Domain SME             lending · SBA · banking operations
   │  defect, data, or policy gap
   ▼
L3 Engineering / Product  bug, incident, roadmap
   │  revenue, reputation, or examiner risk
   ▼
Executive                 Head of Support + CS + Credit

Special paths

VIPP1 / exec-originated
Skip L1. Assign named support owner and CSM. Head of Support stays on the thread until first customer update.
Compliancecomplaint / fair lending
Open a parallel Compliance ticket. Do not wait on L3. No AI-authored reply.
Crediteligibility
Support does not invent a credit decision. Conference Credit. Point at the article, then hand off.

Required fields on every escalate

  • From level → to level
  • Customer impact in one sentence
  • What was already tried
  • Named owner
  • Next update time (never “soon”)

Aging rule: no open VIP escalation older than 48 hours without an exec note.
