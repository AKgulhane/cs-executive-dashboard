Metric definitions

SLA
Service level agreement. Percentage of cases that met first-response and resolution targets for the contracted priority.
Cases handled
Cases touched or closed in the reporting period, plus the live open / in-progress backlog.
MTTR
Mean time to resolution. Average elapsed hours from case open to case close.
Escalations
Cases moved above the standard queue because of severity, SLA risk, or executive attention.
Maintenance window
Agreed period when a change may reduce availability. These hours are typically excluded from SLA clocks.
