Customer FAQ (help center)

How do I reset a portal password?

Use Forgot password on the login page. Bank admins can also reset from Admin → Users. After three failed attempts the account locks for 15 minutes. This should not need a ticket.

Where is my loan in underwriting?

Open Pipeline and select the loan. Status values: Draft, Submitted, In underwriting, Credit decision, Docs requested, Ready to close, Booked. The timestamp is the last human action.

What documents do you need for owner-occupied CRE?

Entity docs, debt schedule, two years of business tax returns, YTD financials, insurance binder, and personal PFS for guarantors. Investor CRE also needs a rent roll.

Should this file be 7(a) or 504?

7(a) is typically working capital, acquisition, and refinance. 504 is major fixed assets via a CDC and the bank. This is a chooser, not an approval. Ask L2 SBA if the use of proceeds is mixed or this is a change of ownership.

Who do I call if a live close is blocked?

Phone the P1 line for your contact region. VIP banks skip the L1 queue. You will get a named owner and a next update time within 15 minutes (10 minutes for VIP), in your local hours.

What are support hours in APAC, EMEA, and AMER?

AMER: Mon–Fri 08:00–20:00 ET. EMEA: Mon–Fri 08:00–20:00 CET. APAC: Mon–Fri 08:00–20:00 SGT. P1 is 24/7 and is routed to the on-call in your region first.
