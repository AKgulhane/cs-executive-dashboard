SLAs and metrics

Written targets for first response, resolution, CSAT, and deflection. Clocks pause only for documented reasons.

Priority policies

VIP banks are one step tighter on first response. P1 uses clock minutes. P3–P4 use business minutes in the customer’s contact region, not a single ET clock.

Clock rules

  • Start first-response clock on create.
  • Pause only for customer-pending, bank-core / E-Tran wait, or announced maintenance.
  • P1 cannot sit in pending without an incident or customer-pause event.
  • 80% of SLA → page assignee and lead.
  • Breach → Head of Support on P1; Team Lead on P2.

P1 is 24/7 on-call in the customer’s region first.
