Hire and train

Hiring bar

Banking or fintech support preferred. Written English that a credit officer will trust. Comfort on the phone with a bank EVP.

Academy (10 days, unsupervised chat only after pass)

  • Days 1–2 — product tour, ticket hygiene, SLA clocks
  • Days 3–5 — commercial lending language, document sets
  • Days 6–8 — SBA 7(a) vs 504, what Support must never decide
  • Days 9–10 — VIP tone, complaints, shadow P1

Leadership cadence

  • Daily 15-min standup on P1, SLA risk, VIP
  • Twice-weekly ticket reviews (quality, not speed)
  • Weekly CSAT / FRT / deflection review
  • Monthly staffing vs. bank count and mix

Early days: Head of Support personally owns every P1 and every executive-originated ticket.
